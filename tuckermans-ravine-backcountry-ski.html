Tuckerman’s Ravine: Sherburne Trail backcountry ski

Friday, March 19–Sunday, March 21, 2027

Activity: Backcountry skiing / ski mountaineering

Difficulty: 5 – Ski Mountaineering

Tour leader: Alex

Format: Volunteer-led

About the tour

Join us for a weekend at Tuckerman’s Ravine, the classic of East Coast backcountry skiing. This tour requires expert ski skills and healthy hiking ability. Expect variable snow and a demanding mountain environment.

The difficulty rating indicates that participants should have the fitness and experience to sustain a moderate pace, be active for 4–6 hours per day, and be confident skiing black-diamond terrain in variable snow. Reasonable breaks are planned.

Schedule and meeting details

  • Start: Friday, March 19, 2027; evening
  • End: Sunday, March 21, 2027; 4:00pm
  • Start and end locations: Details to be announced

Equipment

  • AT skis or Telemark skis with climbing skins
  • Medium-sized backpack (18–35 L)
  • Sunglasses and ski helmet
  • Water bottle or thermos
  • Two pairs of socks
  • Two pairs of gloves: one for climbing and one for the descent
  • See the mountaineering checklist for the full gear list

AT or Telemark ski gear with skins may be rentable; rental location and details have not yet been provided.

Clothing and food

Dress in layers suitable for hiking in the mountains, and bring extra dry layers for skiing down. Bring lunch or enough calories for a long day. If the weather permits, we’ll stop for a picnic; otherwise, we’ll eat along the way.

Communication and registration

Registration details are not yet available. A WhatsApp link will be provided once registered.
