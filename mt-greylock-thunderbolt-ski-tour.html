Mt. Greylock - Thunderbolt Trail ski tour

February 5-6, 2027

Activity type: Ski touring

Trip rating: Vigorous

Audience: Intermediate

Participant minimum: 4

Participant maximum: 6

Leader: Alex de Chazal

Overview

This is a reconnaissance trip to gather information for upcoming tours. We will climb the Thunderbolt Trail to summit Mt. Greylock, conditions permitting. The route is a challenging climb with variable snow and a narrow descent, and the peak is exposed to wind.

Meeting location

The Williams Inn, Williamstown

Meet time: 8:00am on Saturday, February 5

Trip start: evening of February 5

End date/time: February 6, around 3:00pm

Route and itinerary

We will climb up the Thunderbolt Trail. This is a reconnaissance tour to gather information for upcoming tours.

  • Total distance: 3.2 miles
  • Total elevation gain/loss: 2,000 ft
  • Starting elevation: not specified
  • Peak elevation: 3,149 ft

We’ll start early at 8:30am and head up the classic trail. There will be regular rest stops, but everyone should bring snacks and water. Approximately 1.5 liters of water is a good target.

Goals and considerations

Trip goals: Summit Mt. Greylock, conditions permitting.

Rating comments: Challenging climb, variable snow, narrow descent.

Additional resources: There is a good documentary on YouTube.

Alternate / bail-out options: I am not currently aware of any bail-out options. The peak is very exposed to wind, but there is a shelter. If we encounter a problem, we need to work together to exit the mountain as appropriate.
